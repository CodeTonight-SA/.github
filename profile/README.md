CodeTonight-SA builds GRIP, HAL, HAPPI and GRASP.

## GRIP

GRIP turns rules into code that runs.
It checks what it knows first, tests itself and [publishes the score](https://codetonight.co.za/grip-rsi-witness/).
GRIP is private today.

## HAL

HAL works with models from many providers, with a judge who is not the author.
HAL is private today.

## HAPPI

HAPPI is one file you can run.
The current version is 1.6.

## GRASP

GRASP lets you check a record without us.
The [public decision chain](https://codetonight.co.za/grip-decision-chain/) shows its records and Bitcoin stamps.

## What the record does not prove

A verifiable record does not prove that a decision was correct or that an improvement will work elsewhere.
A Bitcoin stamp shows that a record existed by that time, and nothing more.

## What is open and what is private

GRASP is open for inspection, and HAPPI is available through its public site.
GRIP and HAL are private today.
